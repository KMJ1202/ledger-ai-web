What you get

Your public booking page

One link — on your Google profile, your site, your texts. Customers request a time with no account and no app; requests land in your Leads lane and on your phone, ready to confirm.

Bookings that check the calendar first

When Ledger drafts an appointment, it checks Google Calendar for a real conflict and blocks the double-booking unless you deliberately override. The oldest scheduling embarrassment, gone.

Appointments that carry their own billing

Every booking drafted through Ledger carries the services and prices in the event itself — so when the job's done, “invoice this appointment” bills correctly in one step.

A leads pipeline with a call list

New, contacted, quoted, booked — with follow-up dates that surface as a due-now call list. Estimated values roll into a live pipeline number, so you know what's on the table this month.

To-dos that keep score

The small stuff — callbacks, parts to order, that thing you promised — tracked in the same place the rest of the operation lives, not on a sticky note.

Reviews handled with dignity

Google reviews land in the app; the copilot drafts a reply in your voice; nothing publishes until you approve every word.
