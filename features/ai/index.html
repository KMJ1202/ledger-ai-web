What you get

Ask anything about the operation

“How's my day look?” “Sales this month vs last?” “Where's my crew right now?” “Any quotes approved?” “Who forgot to clock out?” — one chat, live answers across the whole business.

It acts, with your say-so

Drafts invoices, estimates, bookings, emails, review replies, payment reminders, client links, and crew dispatches — every external action lands as a confirm card first. You are always the last tap.

It talks to your crew for you

“Tell Dave he's on the 2 o'clock” — it pulls Dave's assignment and email from the roster, writes the dispatch, and sends on your confirm. Day sheets go out automatically every morning.

Business memory that compounds

Tell it your rates, your policies, your suppliers, how you like things done — it remembers permanently and applies it in every future conversation. Live numbers always come fresh; only the how-you-run-things gets remembered.

Advisor Mode

Flip a switch and it's your consultant — pricing, marketing, hiring, growth — grounded in your actual sales and customer data instead of generic internet advice.

Voice, when your hands are full

Hold the mic and talk to it like a person. Same brain, same live data, same confirm-before-acting discipline.

A voice that treats you like the owner

Briefings lead with the headline number, celebrate real wins, and deliver rough news straight — with the next move attached. Executive summaries, not chatbot filler.

Honest to a fault

If a number isn't in your systems, it says so. If a connector is down, it tells you plainly. It will not guess, pad, or invent — by design.

What a morning with it looks like
1

“Morning — how do we look?”

Yesterday's sales, who's booked today, who's dispatched, what's overdue, and anything a customer approved overnight — one briefing, live numbers, ten seconds.

2

“Tell the crew about the schedule change”

It drafts the dispatch emails from the roster; you confirm; they're informed. No group-text archaeology.

3

“Invoice the two finished jobs”

Real customers, real items, live prices, two confirm cards. Done before your coffee's cold.

4

“Anything I should worry about?”

It flags the overdue account creeping up, the crewman still on the clock at 8 PM, the quote that's been sitting unapproved for a week — the things owners find out too late, surfaced early.

The fine print, in plain sight

The discipline underneath the brains.

  • ✓Grounded in your systems. Every figure comes from a live read of your books, calendar, or records — never from the model's imagination.
  • ✓Confirm-first on all writes. Nothing external — invoice, email, booking, reply — happens without your tap.
  • ✓Your memory is yours. Review, edit, pin, or delete anything it has learned about your business, any time, from the memory screen.
  • ✓Scoped like a professional. It runs your business. It politely declines to write homework essays.
