What you get

A real dedicated business number

Powered by carrier-grade calling under the hood, wired straight into the app. Calls and texts land in one feed — no separate phone app to check, no missed notification buried in your personal line.

Same-second missed-call auto-text

The moment a call goes unanswered, the caller gets a text back automatically — before you'd have even finished wiping your hands. Nobody who calls your shop hears dead air again.

Caller, auto-saved as a lead

Every missed call becomes a contact and a lead in the same motion — no typing a number off a call log. It's sitting in your pipeline, ready for a callback, by the time you pick your phone back up.

Business-hours and after-hours templates

Two reply templates, your words — one for during the day, one for nights and weekends. Pick your open days right on the settings card and Ledger sends the right message automatically, every time.

Voicemail, with Call Intelligence

Voicemails play right in the feed the moment they land. Turn on Call Intelligence and every message gets a written transcript beside it, so you can scan what someone needs without hitting play.

Keep the number you already have

No porting, no waiting on a carrier. Dial your carrier's conditional-forwarding code once from your existing phone and missed calls route straight into Ledger — your number never changes.

Or request a dedicated new one

One short form — business name, hours, an optional reply template — and a new business number is provisioned and live, ready to put on your website, your Google profile, and your invoices.

A voicemail greeting, written for you

Ledger drafts a greeting script in your business's voice, ready to read straight into your carrier's recorder — no staring at a blank page trying to sound professional on the fifth take.

Keep your number, or get a new one

No porting delay either way.

Forward your existing line with a carrier code your phone already understands, or request a brand-new dedicated number from inside the app. Both are live the same day.

Conditional call forwarding — dial once, done

BusyEnable *67*<your Ledger number># · Disable #67#
No answerEnable *61*<your Ledger number># · Disable #61#
Unreachable / offEnable *62*<your Ledger number># · Disable #62#
All callsEnable *21*<your Ledger number># · Disable #21#
Works on Bell, Rogers, Telus, Fido, Koodo, AT&T and T-Mobile — dial the enable code once from your existing phone, no app and no carrier settings menu. On Verizon, use your carrier's own call-forwarding settings instead. Prefer a clean break? Skip forwarding entirely and put your new Ledger number on your website, Google profile, invoices and vehicles going forward.
