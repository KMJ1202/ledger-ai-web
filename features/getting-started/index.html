Getting started

Set up Ledger in seven short steps.

Answer a few plain questions about your business — what you do, how you charge, when you're open. Skip any of them; only the kind of business is required. Ledger turns your answers into your price list, your hours and your first working day, and its AI, voice assistant and Front Desk all read the same brief.

How joining works

1

Create your account

Email and password. Your 14-day free trial starts now — no card.

2

Confirm your email

One tap on the link we send.

3

Name your business

Business name and currency. That's the whole form.

4

Answer seven short steps

Most are a tap. Skip any of them and finish later from Settings.

5

Verify your phone

Switches on your AI credit for the trial. No card.

Then Ledger shows your first working day — the next steps for your business, picked from what you told it. Change any answer later in Settings under What Ledger knows.

The seven steps

1

About your business

What kind of business, how long you've been at it, team size, where you're based.

2

What you offer

What you sell or do, with prices. How you charge, and whether you quote first.

3

Your customers and how work comes in

Who you serve, how they reach you, where the work happens.

4

Your week

When you're open, what happens after hours, how soon you can take a job.

5

Getting paid

How customers pay, when payment is due, whether you take a deposit.

6

Your team and tools

Who's on the team, whether Ledger answers missed calls and texts, QuickBooks and Google Calendar.

7

How Ledger should work for you

How it should sound, what matters this year, rules it must always follow, what it may do on its own.
