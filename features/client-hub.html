Platform // Client Hub

Every customer gets their own portal.

One tap in the app creates a personal, private page for any customer: their live balance, every invoice with full line detail, quotes with a big Approve button, their upcoming appointments, and their whole service history. They open it in any browser — no account, no password, no app.

What you get

A live balance, not a statement

The moment they open their link, they see exactly what's owing — pulled live from your books, never a stale PDF. Paid up? They get the green zero and a thank-you.

Every invoice, itemized

Open and paid invoices with full line detail, due dates, and status — customers stop calling to ask “can you resend that invoice?” because it's always in their pocket.

Quotes approved with one tap

Send a quote, and it appears on their page with a big Approve button. One tap records a timestamped, binding approval in your audit trail — and the AI announces it to you as money greenlit. No more chasing signatures by text.

Their appointments, their history

Upcoming visits with tap-to-navigate addresses, plus their full service history — every past job, itemized. Their whole relationship with your shop, on one page.

Book the next job right there

A Book Service button feeds your public booking page — the portal doesn't just answer questions, it brings the next job in.

Instant kill switch

Every link is private to one customer and revocable in one tap. Regenerate it and the old link dies instantly. Wrong link? It shows nothing — ever.

The AI runs it for you

“Make a client link for Calgary Metal and send it” — the copilot finds the exact customer (it even catches duplicate records and asks), mints the link, and drafts the email for your one-tap send.

Your privacy rules, enforced by the server

Cost, supplier, and internal information physically never reaches the page — the server only ever sends customer-safe fields. What they see is exactly what belongs on their side of the counter.

How it works
1

Create the link in one tap

Open any customer in the app — the Client Hub card is right on their profile. Tap create, and their personal page exists. QR, copy, and share buttons are right there.

2

Send it once

Text or email it — or tell the AI to draft the send. The customer saves it; from then on their answers live in their pocket instead of your phone line.

3

Quotes get approved while you sleep

They open the quote, read every line, tap Approve. You get a timestamped record and a headline in your next briefing: money greenlit.

4

The page stays true automatically

Balance, invoices, appointments, history — all live. There is nothing to update, resend, or keep in sync. It reads your systems in real time.

The fine print, in plain sight

Straight answers about the fine print.

  • ✓Legally clean. A customer viewing their own invoices and history is exactly what privacy law permits; tap-to-approve quotes are binding under electronic transactions law, recorded with a timestamp.
  • ✓Scoped hard. Each link shows one customer their own data and nothing else. Tokens are unguessable and revocable.
  • ✓No seat fees, no customer accounts. The incumbents gate their customer portal behind their highest tiers. Ours is included — for every customer.
  • ✓Approvals can't double-fire. A second tap on Approve is a no-op; the first timestamp stands.
