What you get

One link is their whole setup

Add a crew member — name, phone, email, a color. They get a personal magic link (with a QR code to scan right off your screen). No app store, no password, no training session. They save it to their home screen and they're operational.

Morning day sheets, automatically

Pick an hour, and every crew member gets their day emailed automatically: every job with its time, address, notes, and a NAVIGATE button. Their whole route, in their inbox, before they finish their coffee.

One-tap dispatch

Assign someone to a job and hit the dispatch button — they get a clean branded email with the job, the time, the address, gate codes and notes, and their live-day link. Or just tell the AI: “tell Dave he's on the 2 o'clock.”

Live status you can see from anywhere

On their page, crew tap On my way → On site → Done as they work. Every tap lands on your calendar as a colored chip, instantly. Ask the AI “where's my crew right now?” and get a real answer — no phone tag.

Turn-by-turn to every job

Every job carries a glowing NAVIGATE button — Apple Maps turn-by-turn on iPhone, Google Maps on Android, one tap and they're driving to the customer. Phone numbers in the job notes are tap-to-call.

A real punch clock

Big CLOCK IN / CLOCK OUT on their page with a live ticking timer. Punches are stamped by the server — nobody can type their own hours. Lunch is just out-and-back-in.

Time cards built for payroll

Daily and weekly hours per crew member, who's on the clock right now, overtime split automatically under Alberta's rules (over 8h/day or 44h/week), and a one-tap CSV export for whoever runs your payroll.

Corrections with a paper trail

Someone forgot to clock out? Fix the punch in seconds — the original tap is never erased. Your correction sits beside it with a note, timestamped, which is exactly what keeps the record payroll-legal.

The AI knows your crew

“How many hours did Dave work this week?” “Is anyone still clocked in?” “Who's dispatched tomorrow?” — the copilot reads dispatch, status, and time cards live, and flags a forgotten clock-out before it becomes a payroll headache.

A crew member's day, start to finish
1

They get one link — once

Text it, email it, or let them scan the QR off your screen. It opens a dark, fast page with their name on it. If a phone is lost or someone moves on, tap Regenerate — the old link dies that second.

2

Morning: day sheet lands, they clock in

Their route arrives by email at the hour you choose. At the shop, they open their page and tap CLOCK IN — the timer starts, stamped server-side.

3

All day: navigate, work, tap status

NAVIGATE to each job, then On my way → On site → Done as the work happens. You watch chips change color on your calendar instead of making calls.

4

End of day: clock out — payroll's already done

Their hours are on your Time Cards screen the moment they tap: daily totals, weekly totals, overtime split, exportable in one tap.

The fine print, in plain sight

What this costs per crew member: nothing.

  • ✓No per-seat fees, ever. Five crew or fifteen — the price doesn't move. The incumbents charge per user, monthly, forever, for exactly this.
  • ✓No app for the crew. Their link works in any phone browser. Nothing to install, update, or forget the password for.
  • ✓Punches can't be fudged. Times are stamped by the server, not the phone. Corrections are owner-only and keep the original on record.
  • ✓No GPS tracking of people. Status and punches are taps your crew make themselves. Hours tracking for payroll — not surveillance.
  • ✓Alberta-ready records. Employment standards require hours-of-work records — the time cards are that record, with a proper amendment trail.
