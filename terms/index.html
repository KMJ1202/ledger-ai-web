Terms of Service

Effective September 2, 2026 · Ledger AI, Calgary, Alberta, Canada

These are the rules for using Ledger AI. They're written in plain English on purpose. By creating a workspace or using the app, you agree to them. If you don't, please don't use Ledger AI.

What Ledger AI is

Ledger AI is a business copilot for small businesses. It connects to tools you already use — QuickBooks Online, Google Calendar, Gmail, Google Business Profile, Stripe — and answers questions, drafts documents and takes actions on your data.

  • You stay in charge. Anything that leaves your workspace — an invoice, an email, a booking, a payment link — is shown to you first and only goes out when you confirm it.
  • It is not an accountant, lawyer or tax advisor. Ledger AI works from the numbers in your books and can make mistakes. Check anything that matters before you rely on it, especially tax and legal questions.

Your account

  • You must be at least 18 and authorized to act for the business you sign up.
  • Keep your sign-in email secure. Anyone with access to your inbox can sign in to your workspace.
  • Team members you invite act under your workspace. You're responsible for who you invite and what they do.
  • One workspace per business. You may not share, resell or sublicense your access.

Your data

Your data is yours. We only use it to run the features you turn on, exactly as described in our Privacy Policy. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and never train AI models on it.

  • Every connection (QuickBooks, Google, Stripe) requires your explicit consent and can be disconnected at any time from Business profile & settings.
  • Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • You can delete your workspace and everything in it at any time. Deletion is permanent.

Free trial, billing and cancellation

  • New workspaces get a 14-day free trial. No card is needed to start.
  • After the trial, Ledger AI is a monthly subscription billed through Stripe. The price is shown on our pricing page and in the app before you add a card.
  • There are no per-seat fees, and your business phone number is included in the plan, along with $25 of texting credit each month, metered at our carrier's actual per-message cost. Unused texting credit resets on the 1st and does not carry over. Add-ons (like extra AI usage or a $25 texting top-up) are shown with their price before you buy them, and top-up credit is added the moment the payment clears.
  • Cancel any time from Business profile & settings → Subscription & billing. You keep access until the end of the period you've paid for. We don't give partial-month refunds.
  • If a payment fails, we'll tell you and give you time to fix it before anything is turned off.
  • Prices can change. We'll email you at least 30 days before any change takes effect.

Acceptable use

Use Ledger AI for running your own legitimate business. Don't use it to:

  • send spam or messages people haven't agreed to receive;
  • break any law, including tax, privacy and consumer-protection law;
  • access data you don't have the right to access;
  • probe, overload or reverse-engineer the service.

We can suspend or close a workspace that breaks these rules. We'll tell you why unless the law prevents it.

QuickBooks, Google, Stripe and our phone provider have their own terms, and connecting them means you also agree to those. If one of them changes or shuts off an integration, we'll do our best to adapt, but we can't guarantee any third-party service stays available.

What we promise, and what we don't

  • We work hard to keep Ledger AI fast, accurate and available, and we back up your data daily.
  • Ledger AI is provided "as is". AI answers can be wrong. We aren't liable for decisions you make based on them, or for losses from outages, third-party failures or events outside our control.
  • To the extent the law allows, our total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim.

Changes and governing law

We may update these terms. If a change matters, we'll email you before it takes effect; continuing to use Ledger AI after that means you accept it. These terms are governed by the laws of Alberta, Canada.
