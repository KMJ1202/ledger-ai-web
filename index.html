Ledger OS // Inventory & pricing

Upload your price list. Ledger sells from it.

Any spreadsheet, any column names. Ledger reads the header, works out what's what, and from then on it quotes, checks stock and drafts estimates from your numbers — never a guess.

1

Choose a file

In the app: Your business → Inventory & pricing → Choose a file. CSV or tab-separated, straight out of Excel, Google Sheets or your supplier. No template to fill in first.

2

Ledger maps your columns

It reads the header row and figures out which column is the item, brand, size, price, cost and in-stock count — even when they're called “Desc”, “Sell $” or “QOH”. You see the mapping and a five-row preview. Fix anything, then tap Import.

3

Now it sells

“Do we have 265/70R17?” “What's the price on the Wildpeak?” “Quote Sarah four of them.” Ledger answers from your list — price, stock count, as-of date — and drafts the estimate or the text reply. Replace the file whenever prices change; nothing stale gets quoted.

Ledger OS // Capabilities

An employee who never sleeps

Not a chatbot with opinions — a copilot wired into the tools you already run your business on, live.

Type it, or talk to it

Ask in plain text, or hold the mic and just talk — live voice conversation, not a script. "Sales this month?" "Who's overdue?" "What's booked Monday?" Answers come from your real numbers, never made up.

Receipt Radar

Connect your inbox and Ledger watches it for you — supplier bills and receipts get pulled out, read for vendor, total, GST and date, and queued for your review. Nothing posts to QuickBooks until you confirm the category.

Photo receipts

No inbox trail? Snap a picture of a paper receipt and it joins the exact same review-then-post pipeline as your email receipts — vendor, tax, and total read automatically.

Invoicing, drafted and printed

Ledger drafts the invoice or estimate from the conversation; you tap Confirm before anything posts to QuickBooks. Ask it to print any invoice or estimate by number and it pulls the document straight from your books.

Built-in books & card payments

No QuickBooks? Ledger runs your invoicing on its own built-in books — numbered, taxed invoices with a personal payment page for every customer. Connect Stripe once and they pay by card online; the invoice marks itself paid the moment the money lands.

Estimates customers answer in one tap

Every estimate gets a share page — your customer opens the link, sees the quote, and taps Accept or Decline. Accepted? Convert it to a numbered invoice in one tap. Nothing touches your books until then.

Bookings that actually check your calendar

Ledger drafts the appointment, checks Google Calendar for a real time conflict before it books, and blocks the double-booking unless you deliberately override it. Night-before reminder texts go out automatically, and every appointment can carry one.

Missed calls, recovered automatically

A dedicated business number auto-texts anyone who calls and can't reach you, saves them as a lead, and plays their voicemail right in the feed. The whole phone story →

Who-owes-you-money watch

Open invoices, overdue amounts, and the biggest ones coming due — surfaced the moment you ask, pulled live from QuickBooks, never a stale export.

Google review replies

Ledger reads your Google Business reviews and drafts a reply in your voice. It never publishes on its own — you approve every word before it goes live.

Business memory that learns

Tell it a rate you charge, a policy, a supplier — it remembers, permanently, and uses it in every future conversation. Live numbers always come fresh from your books; only the facts about how you run things get remembered.

Crew dispatch & live links

Assign a job and dispatch it in one tap — your crew get a personal link with the route, turn-by-turn navigation, and On my way → On site → Done taps that land on your calendar live. The whole crew story →

Punch clock & time cards

Crew clock in and out on their own link — server-stamped, correction-safe, with overtime split automatically to your rules and a one-tap payroll export. See time cards →

Client Hub portals

Every customer gets a personal magic link — live balance, itemized invoices, quotes they approve with one tap, appointments, and history. Inside the Client Hub →

Advisor Mode

Flip one switch and it becomes your business consultant — marketing, pricing, hiring, growth — grounded in your actual sales and customer data, not generic tips.
