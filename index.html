Ledger OS // Straight answers

The questions that stop a $699 decision.

Answered the way we'd answer them across the counter.

Does it replace my bookkeeper or accountant?
No, and it isn't trying to. Ledger reads your live books so you can ask questions and get things done without waiting — it doesn't do your year-end. Most owners keep their bookkeeper and hand them cleaner books: receipts filed the day they arrive, invoices that actually went out, nothing missing at month-end.
What if I don't use QuickBooks?
You can still run everything. Ledger has its own built-in books — invoices, customers, receipts, who owes you — so you can start without QuickBooks and connect it later if you ever want to. If you're already on QuickBooks Online, you connect it once and Ledger works from your real numbers.
Who can see my books and my customers?
You, and anyone you invite as a teammate. That's it. We never sell your data, never use it for ads, and never train AI models on it. No Ledger staff reads your books or email as a matter of course — only if you ask us for help with something specific, and with your knowledge. Every connection can be cut off by you at any time from inside the app under Connections — QuickBooks, Gmail and Google alike (QuickBooks can also be cut from your Intuit account's connected apps). The full plain-English version is in our Privacy Policy.
Can it do something I didn't ask for?
No. Every invoice, booking, review reply, receipt post and text is drafted first and waits for your tap. Nothing leaves your business without your confirm. The only things that go out on their own are the lanes you switch on yourself — the missed-call text-back, the night-before reminder, and Front Desk, which answers texts and voicemails for you when you can't and shows you every reply in the Phone tab. All three are off until you turn them on, and you write the first two messages yourself.
How does the free trial work?
Create your account for a 14-day software trial, no card required. Try the built-in books and a scripted phone walkthrough. Live calling/texting requires a paid subscription, an available number and provider activation; Front Desk requires Pro. Connected services need their own setup. On day 14 you decide: add a card and keep going, or do nothing and nothing is charged. If you keep it and later leave, cancel anytime from Business profile & settings → Subscription & billing — you keep access to the end of the month you've paid for.
How long does setup take, really?
Create your account and connect QuickBooks — or skip it and use the built-in books: under ten minutes, and you're asking questions. Booking and crew setup can be started from Business profile & settings. Phone activation is usually the same business day after subscribing, but number availability and carrier checks can take longer. Complete these steps there — or set them up on the optional free 30-minute onboarding call with the founder, usually in your first day or two. There's no setup fee and no consultant.
What does the AI allowance mean? Will I get surprise bills?
Ledger Solo includes $60 a month of AI use and $10 a month of texting on your business number. Ledger Pro includes $150 a month of AI use and $25 a month of texting. A question to Ledger costs about 11¢ on average, so $60 is roughly 550 questions a month and $150 roughly 1,400. Both meters are visible in the app. Texting usage depends on message length, destination and carrier charges. Any top-up is shown with its price before you buy it; nothing is added automatically. See exactly how AI usage works →
Does it work on Android, or on my desk computer?
Yes. Ledger runs on iPhone, Android (through the web app) and any desktop browser — same account, same books. Your crew and customers don't install anything at all: they get a link.
