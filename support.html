Support

Ledger AI, Calgary, Alberta, Canada

Get in touch

Email us anytime: supportteam@heyledger.ai

We aim to reply within 1 business day.

What to include

To get you sorted faster, please include:

  • The email address your Ledger AI account is registered under.
  • What you were doing when the issue happened (e.g. "asking about overdue invoices," "connecting QuickBooks," "photographing a receipt").
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if you have one — it helps a lot.

Common requests

  • Reconnect QuickBooks, Gmail, or Calendar — go to Settings → Connections in the app, or ask us and we'll help.
  • Billing / subscription questions — email us; we can look up your Stripe subscription status.
  • Delete your account — Settings → Delete account in the app, or email us and we'll remove it for you. This is permanent and removes your workspace, receipts, conversations, and connections.
