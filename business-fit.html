What a file import means

  • Choose customers, products/inventory or a service menu. Preview the destination, worksheet, columns and exceptions before saving.
  • Use one table with a header row. Excel, CSV and tab-separated formats are supported within the size and row limits shown by the app. This is not a promise that every spreadsheet layout will work.
  • Customer extra columns are preserved as notes and original fields, not automatically turned into specialist records. Service imports require reviewed prices; bookable services also need valid durations.
  • A range or bundle such as “10–20” or “2 for $10” is not a settled unit price. Resolve flagged values before using them in a quote.
  • Download original imports and catalog history. Eligible customer-only imports can be undone if later edits or linked records would not be lost. A business archive is not a one-click whole-business restore.

Before the first real job

  1. Set your business details, time zone and hours.
  2. Import a small representative file and check the actual saved contacts, notes and prices.
  3. Review service duration, taxable/exempt treatment and any staff/resource constraints.
  4. Review tax registration and rates explicitly. A location is not proof that your business should charge a particular tax.
  5. Create a reviewed appointment and invoice for a real intended job. Check every line and recipient before confirming or sending.
  6. Test your connected providers separately. A saved payment record does not prove funds settled; an accepted text does not prove delivery.
