Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · Ledger AI, Calgary, Alberta, Canada

Ledger AI is a business copilot: it connects to the tools you already run your business on and answers questions about your own data. This page explains, plainly, what we collect, why, where it lives, who else touches it, and how to get rid of it.

What we collect, and why

We only collect what's needed to run the features you turn on. Every connection below requires your explicit consent — nothing connects itself.

  • Account & billing — your name, email, and subscription status, so you can sign in and we know your plan is active. Card details are handled entirely by Stripe; they never reach our servers.
  • QuickBooks Online — invoices, customers, and sales/P&L data, so the assistant can answer questions about your books and, when you ask it to, draft invoices or expenses. Nothing posts to QuickBooks without your confirmation.
  • Gmail — read access to find supplier receipt and invoice emails for the Receipt Radar feature, so incoming costs get captured automatically instead of you forwarding them by hand.
  • Google Calendar — read and create access, so the assistant can tell you what's booked and add appointments you ask it to schedule.
  • Google Business Profile — your business reviews, so the assistant can surface and help you respond to them.
  • Receipt photos — images you snap of paper receipts, stored privately and accessed only through signed, time-limited URLs.
  • Conversations with the assistant — what you type or say, plus the business data needed to answer it, so the assistant can hold a conversation and remember context within a session.
  • Your customers and crew — the names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, vehicles, invoices, appointments, hours worked and clock-in times you enter or that arrive through your booking page, phone line and crew links. This is your business's data about the people you serve and employ; we hold and process it only on your instructions and you're responsible for collecting it lawfully.
  • Business phone — if you use a Ledger-provided number or forward your own line: call logs, caller numbers, text messages in both directions, voicemail recordings and their written transcripts, so missed calls, texts and voicemails show up in the app.
  • Vehicle lookups — a VIN or licence-plate you scan or type is sent to a vehicle-data service to identify the year, make, model and tire sizes. Nothing else about the customer goes with it.
  • Inventory & price lists — the spreadsheets you upload, so the assistant can quote from your own numbers.
  • Business memory — facts you tell the assistant to remember (rates, policies, suppliers), kept until you change or delete them.
  • Push notifications — a device token for your iPhone or browser, so we can alert you when a customer texts, a review lands or a job needs cover. You can turn these off in your device settings.
  • Usage metering — token counts and cost per request, plus text-message counts, so we can enforce your plan's monthly AI and texting allowances.
  • Cookies and analytics — none. We don't run advertising trackers or third-party analytics on our website or in the app; the only thing stored in your browser is what keeps you signed in.

Where it lives

Your account data, receipts, and conversation history are stored with Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage) in their Canadian region (Montréal), so your business data stays in Canada at rest. Some processors below run outside Canada — the AI providers, Stripe and the phone carrier are US-based — and receive only what's needed for the feature in use. Receipt images are kept in private storage and served only through signed URLs that expire — they are not publicly accessible.

How we protect it

Sensitive data — including everything we receive from Google APIs (Gmail message content, Calendar events, and Business Profile reviews) — is protected by the following controls:

  • Encrypted in transit. All traffic between your browser or app, Ledger AI, and every connected service runs over TLS (HTTPS). We do not accept unencrypted connections.
  • Encrypted at rest. Your database records, receipt images, and stored files are encrypted at rest by our hosting provider, Supabase.
  • Google tokens are held in an encrypted vault, never in the app. Your Google access and refresh tokens are written to Supabase Vault, which stores them encrypted. Our database keeps only a reference to the vault entry — never the token itself. The functions that read a token are server-side and privileged: they are explicitly revoked from every browser and app session, so a signed-in client cannot retrieve your Google credentials even if it asks for them.
  • Your workspace is isolated. Row-level security is enabled on every tenant table and every policy is scoped to your workspace, so one customer's Gmail, Calendar, or business data can never be returned to another customer's account.
  • Least privilege. We request the narrowest Google scopes each feature needs, and only after your explicit OAuth consent. Gmail access is read-only for Receipt Radar; Calendar access is limited to reading and creating events you ask for.
  • You can revoke us at any time. Removing Ledger AI at myaccount.google.com/permissions immediately ends our access to your Google data.
  • Limited human access. No Ledger AI staff member reads your Gmail, Calendar, or business data as a matter of course. It happens only where needed to resolve a support request you have raised, with your knowledge.
  • No training, no ads, no resale. Google user data is never used to train AI models, never used for advertising, and never sold or transferred to anyone beyond the named processors required to operate the features you switch on.
  • Deletion removes the credentials too. Deleting your account deletes your stored Google tokens out of the vault along with your workspace data — see Deletion below.

Who processes it

We don't build our own AI models or payment rails — we use a small number of named processors to run the service:

  • Anthropic (Claude models) — receives your conversation content and the business data needed to answer it, as the processor behind the AI assistant's text responses.
  • OpenAI (Realtime API) — receives audio/conversation content when you use voice mode, as the processor behind spoken conversations.
  • Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage.
  • Stripe — processes your subscription payments and, if you turn on card payments, your customers' payments through your own Stripe account. Card numbers never touch Ledger AI's servers.
  • Telnyx — the carrier behind Ledger-provided business numbers: routes calls and texts, stores voicemail recordings and produces the written transcripts.
  • Resend — delivers the emails we send on your behalf (invoices, estimates, sign-in links, receipts).
  • Apple (Push Notification service) and your browser's push service — deliver notifications to your devices. They see the device token and the notification text, nothing else.
  • Wheel-Size and the US NHTSA vehicle database — decode the VIN or plate you look up into year, make, model and tire sizes.
  • Google (Gmail, Calendar, Business Profile) and Intuit (QuickBooks Online) — the source systems you connect; we read from and, where you direct us to, write to their APIs under the OAuth permissions you grant.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data to anyone.
  • We do not use your data to train AI models — yours or anyone else's.
  • We do not share your data beyond the named processors above, and only for the purpose of running the service.

Retention

We keep your workspace data — receipts, conversations, and connected-account data — for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, that data is permanently removed rather than archived. Usage metering records are kept for billing and support purposes for the life of the account. If your subscription ends, access to your workspace is suspended, but your data is kept for at least 90 days so you can export it or come back; after that we may delete it permanently. Business phone numbers are held for 30 days after cancellation and then released.

Deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data two ways:

  • In-app — Settings → Delete account.
  • By email — write to supportteam@heyledger.ai and ask us to delete your account.

Either path permanently removes your workspace, receipts, conversations, and connected-account tokens. This cannot be undone.

Gmail and Google user data

Ledger AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In practice: Gmail access is read-only and used solely to detect supplier receipt/invoice emails for Receipt Radar. We do not use Gmail content for advertising, and we do not let humans read your email except as needed to debug a support request you've raised, with your knowledge.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: supportteam@heyledger.ai.
